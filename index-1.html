Common questions

Can SDG build around my exact workflow or menu?

Yes. Most projects start with a workflow conversation. We plan the line, equipment, refrigeration, and service window around how your team cooks and serves, not the other way around.

Can SDG handle high-volume restaurant, franchise, or event use?

Yes. Frames, electrical, hoods, and equipment are sized for commercial use. We've built trailers running daily restaurant volume and high-traffic event setups.

Can SDG support multi-unit or institutional projects?

Yes. We build repeatable, spec-controlled units for franchises, brands, and institutional buyers. Documentation and consistent build standards are part of the process.

Will the trailer meet compliance and safety requirements?

Builds are planned with health, fire suppression, electrical, and DOT requirements in mind. We provide documentation and specs so your local approval process is straightforward.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on size, equipment, finish, and compliance needs. Once we understand the project, we send a real quote with line items, not a generic number.

How long does a build take?

Timelines depend on complexity and current production load. We give honest delivery windows during the quote conversation, not optimistic guesses.

What happens after I submit the form?

A real SDG builder reviews your request and follows up with relevant examples, layout options, and quote details. No autoresponder loops, no sales script.

Will I get pressured by sales?

No. SDG sells trailers by understanding the operation. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you. If we are, we'll show you exactly why.
